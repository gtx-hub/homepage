FAQ
Security & Trust
Is Assetra a legitimate trading platform?

Yes, Assetra is a Delaware-registered company (USA) operating under proper business registration. We follow KYC/AML regulations and implement bank-level security measures to protect user assets.

How do you protect my funds and personal information?

We use multi-layer security including cold storage for crypto assets, SSL encryption for data transmission, two-factor authentication, and comply with industry-standard security protocols.

Are you regulated?

Assetra operates as a Delaware-incorporated entity in compliance with applicable US regulations. We maintain proper business licensing and follow KYC/AML requirements.

Trading & Fees
What are your trading fees?

We offer competitive, transparent fees with no hidden charges. Our fee structure is clearly displayed on our platform, and you'll see exact costs before confirming any trade.

What cryptocurrencies can I trade?

We support major cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other popular digital assets. Our platform offers various trading pairs with real-time market data.

Do you offer advanced trading tools?

Yes, our platform includes professional features like multiple order types (limit, stop-loss, take-profit), real-time charts, technical indicators, and market analysis tools.

Account & Support

Simply create an account, complete our quick verification process, and you can start trading. The setup process is designed to be fast and user-friendly.

Yes, we offer 24/7 customer support through multiple channels. Our team includes crypto trading experts ready to help with any questions or issues.

Absolutely. Our interface is designed to be intuitive for beginners while offering advanced features for experienced traders. We also provide educational resources and guides.
