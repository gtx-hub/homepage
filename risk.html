RISK DISCLOSURE STATEMENT

IMPORTANT. : Digital asset trading involves substantial financial risk and may result in significant losses, including the total loss of your investment. This Risk Disclosure Statement outlines critical risks you must understand before using Assetra's trading platform.

Market and Price Risks

Extreme Volatility Digital assets exhibit price volatility far exceeding traditional financial instruments:

  • Bitcoin has experienced single-day price swings exceeding 20%
  • Smaller cryptocurrencies can lose 50% or more of their value within hours
  • Market sentiment can shift rapidly based on news, rumors, or social media activity
  • Price movements often occur outside traditional market hours with no circuit breakers

Market Manipulation Digital asset markets may be subject to:

  • Price manipulation by large holders ("whales")
  • Coordinated buying or selling campaigns
  • False or misleading information designed to influence prices
  • "Pump and dump" schemes targeting smaller cryptocurrencies

Liquidity and Execution Risks

Limited Market Depth

  • Some digital assets have thin order books with wide bid-ask spreads
  • Large orders may significantly impact market prices
  • You may not be able to execute trades at expected prices during volatile periods
  • Low-volume assets may be difficult or impossible to sell quickly

Platform Liquidity Risks

  • Trading may be suspended during extreme market conditions
  • System overloads during high-volume periods may delay order execution
  • Connectivity issues may prevent timely trade execution
  • Slippage between order placement and execution can result in unexpected prices

Technology and Security Risks

Blockchain Technology Risks

  • Network congestion can delay transaction confirmations for hours or days
  • Smart contract vulnerabilities may result in permanent asset loss
  • Blockchain forks may create uncertainty about asset ownership
  • Mining attacks could compromise transaction integrity

Platform Security Risks

  • Cyber attacks targeting exchanges have resulted in billions of dollars in losses
  • Phishing attempts and social engineering attacks target user credentials
  • Software bugs or system failures may temporarily prevent access to funds
  • Despite security measures, no system is completely immune to breaches

Regulatory and Legal Risks

Evolving Regulations

  • Government actions can immediately impact digital asset values and accessibility
  • New regulations may restrict or prohibit certain trading activities
  • Tax treatment of digital assets varies by jurisdiction and continues evolving
  • Cross-border regulatory conflicts may affect international trading

Compliance Risks

  • Changes in anti-money laundering requirements may affect account access
  • Know-Your-Customer regulations may require additional documentation
  • Sanctions and prohibited persons lists are updated frequently
  • Failure to comply with regulations may result in account restrictions

Operational and Business Risks

Counterparty Risks

  • Assetra, like all exchanges, faces business risks that could affect operations
  • Regulatory action against Assetra could impact service availability
  • Changes in banking relationships may affect fiat currency services
  • Third-party service providers may experience disruptions

Custody Risks

  • Digital assets held on exchanges face different risks than self-custody
  • Bankruptcy or insolvency proceedings could affect asset recovery
  • Insurance coverage, if any, may not cover all potential losses
  • Key management systems, despite safeguards, present single points of failure

Investment-Specific Risks

Leverage and Margin Trading If you use leveraged products:

  • Losses can exceed your initial investment
  • Margin calls may force liquidation of positions at unfavorable prices
  • Interest charges can accumulate rapidly
  • Market gaps can result in losses beyond posted margin

Concentration Risk

  • Investing heavily in digital assets increases portfolio risk
  • Correlation between different cryptocurrencies can be high during market stress
  • Diversification within digital assets may not provide expected protection
  • Traditional risk management principles may not apply to crypto markets

Information and Decision-Making Risks

Market Information Quality

  • Price data quality varies significantly between platforms and assets
  • Market analysis tools may not account for unique digital asset characteristics
  • Historical data may not be available or reliable for newer assets
  • Social media and online forums contain significant misinformation

Investment Decision Factors Before trading digital assets, carefully consider:

  • Your overall financial situation and ability to withstand losses
  • Investment objectives and time horizon
  • Risk tolerance and experience with volatile investments
  • Percentage of total portfolio allocated to high-risk investments
  • Access to emergency funds for unexpected expenses

Professional Advice Recommendation Consult qualified professionals regarding:

  • Financial advisors for investment suitability assessment
  • Tax professionals for reporting obligations and implications
  • Legal counsel for regulatory compliance in your jurisdiction
  • Estate planning attorneys for digital asset inheritance planning

Assetra's Role and Limitations

No Investment Advice Assetra provides trading infrastructure and tools but does not:

  • Recommend specific investments or trading strategies
  • Guarantee profits or protection against losses
  • Provide personalized financial, tax, or legal advice
  • Endorse any particular digital asset or investment approach

Platform Limitations Despite our best efforts:

  • We cannot prevent all market risks or system failures
  • Insurance coverage may not protect against all potential losses
  • Our risk management systems may not prevent significant losses
  • Past platform performance does not guarantee future reliability

Acknowledgment Required By using Assetra's platform, you acknowledge that:

  • You have read and understand these risks
  • You accept full responsibility for all trading decisions
  • You understand that digital asset trading may not be suitable for your situation
  • You will not invest more than you can afford to lose completely
  • You understand that seeking professional advice is recommended

Emergency Contact Information If you experience significant losses or platform issues:

  • Risk management questions: risk@assetra.xyz
  • Account concerns: support@assetra.xyz

Effective Date: This Risk Disclosure Statement is effective as of 02/28/2025 and may be updated to reflect changing market conditions and regulatory requirements.

Remember: Only invest what you can afford to lose completely. Digital assets should represent only a small portion of a well-diversified investment portfolio.
