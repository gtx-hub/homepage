How do I get started on Assetra?

Getting started is simple and secure. Create your account in minutes, complete our streamlined identity verification process (required for regulatory compliance), fund your account using your preferred method, and begin trading immediately. Our user-friendly interface guides you through each step, and our 24/7 support team is available if you need assistance.

What are your trading fees and how transparent are they?

Assetra offers competitive trading fees starting as low as 0.1% per transaction, with no hidden charges or surprise costs. Our complete fee structure is displayed on your trading interface before you confirm any order, so you know exactly what you'll pay. Volume traders benefit from reduced fees, and we never charge fees for account registration or maintenance.

What cryptocurrencies and trading pairs are available?

Trade popular cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and a carefully selected range of established digital assets. We offer multiple trading pairs including crypto-to-crypto and fiat-to-crypto options, all with real-time market data, professional charts, and deep liquidity to ensure optimal trading conditions.

What is the minimum trade amount on Assetra?

You can start trading with as little as $5, making Assetra accessible to both new traders learning the basics and experienced investors testing strategies. There's no maximum limit, allowing you to scale your trading as your confidence and portfolio grow. This low minimum ensures everyone can participate in the digital asset revolution.

What should I do if I forget my password or lose account access?

Account security is our priority. If you lose access, use our secure "Forgot Password" feature on the login page, or contact our 24/7 support team through live chat or email. We'll verify your identity through multiple security checks and help you regain secure access to your account. Enable two-factor authentication to prevent future access issues and enhance security.
